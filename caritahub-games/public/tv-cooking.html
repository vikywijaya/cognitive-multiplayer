How To Play
🍽️
Watch the Tables
Customers sit at tables waiting for their food! Their mood changes: 😊 patient → 😤 impatient → 😡 furious as time runs out!
👨‍🍳
Head Chef Hints
On your phone, the Head Chef tells you which table needs help most. Pick the task he shouts about!
🎮
Mini-Games
🔪 Chop: tap like crazy!  🥄 Stir: swipe in circles!  👆 Flip: time the green zone!
🏆
Serve 8 Tables!
Finish all 3 steps of an order → the table gets served! First team to serve 8 tables wins!
Work together — each player can handle a different task at the same time!
